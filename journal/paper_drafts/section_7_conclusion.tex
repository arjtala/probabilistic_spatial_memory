\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

\psm is a streaming bounded-memory index for egocentric look-back QA: an H3
hex-grid tessellation with per-cell time-decayed HLL sketches, reservoir-sampled
exemplars, and a \caponek{} knob trading spatial diversity for
embedding-similarity precision, bounded independently of recording duration
(\S\ref{sec:method}; the index runs at sub-2\,ms/query and ${\sim}20$\,MiB on a
Galaxy S22, the CLIP encoder being the remaining on-device cost,
\S\ref{sec:limitations}).

\paragraph{What the evidence supports.}
The bounded $R{=}128$ index tracks brute-force CLIP retrieval monotonically in
\caponek{} and recovers it exactly once eviction is removed at $R{=}1024$
(\S\ref{sec:results-main}), and \psm{}'s query-guided retrieval closes the
temporal-coverage gap frontier MLLMs lack, reaching every street-scale session
uniform-sampling Gemini misses (\S\ref{sec:results-vanilla-mllm},
Fig.~\ref{fig:f2-psm-vs-mllm}). On 102.9\,h of driving (HDD,
\S\ref{sec:results-hdd}) it exercises the multi-session persistence the
single-pass corpus cannot---area-bounded state, cross-session cardinality
accrual over months, and label-free place recognition---the deployment regime
the design targets.

\paragraph{Follow-up directions.}
Immediate extensions: on-device rerank, counting QA over the HLL cardinalities
(\S\ref{sec:results-hdd}), and federated memory via HLL union.
The engine (${\sim}700$ LoC of C) and evaluation harness are released under
MIT.\footnote{Anonymized code for double-blind review:
\url{https://anonymous.4open.science/r/probabilistic_spatial_memory-3C17/}. The
one-shot driver \texttt{scripts/reproduce\_paper.sh} regenerates the headline
figures and Table~\ref{tab:multi-corpus-h3} verdicts from captured artifacts.}
