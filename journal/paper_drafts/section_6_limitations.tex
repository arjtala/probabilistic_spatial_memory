\section{Limitations}
\label{sec:limitations}

\paragraph{Benchmark ceiling and coverage.}
Both \psm{} and brute-force CLIP plateau at 13.4\% Hit@5 on the headline session
(\S\ref{sec:results-main}): 162/187 narrations describe repeatedly-entered rooms
with near-identical frames, so both rarely match the annotator's single chosen
interval---benchmark hardness, not method quality (the two agree on the 25
uniquely-groundable questions; future benchmarks should score against \emph{any}
acceptable revisit window). The deepest single-session analysis (supp.\
Table~\ref{tab:headline}) is one Nymeria session, and the 14-session sweep uses a
heuristic monotonicity-plus-lift criterion, not a formal test.

\paragraph{One honest spatial-axis failure.}
Of the 14 street-scale sessions, \texttt{BurlingameDT4\_feb5} (242\,m bbox) is
the only one where all three encoders fail the monotone-\caponek{} lift---a
visually homogeneous parking-lot street whose queries collapse onto a few
indistinguishable cells. The same failure recurs on Aria Gen~2
Pilot's~\cite{aria-gen2-pilot} GPS-grounded walks, confirming the bottleneck is
encoder discrimination, not trajectory quality.

\paragraph{Evaluation scope.}
\psm's latency (supp.\ \S\ref{sec:supp-memory-latency}) is a host-CPU sanity
check, not an on-device claim (the wearable target is unmeasured, though porting
the C engine is straightforward). Our baselines omit a spatial-only heuristic
(nearest-GPS, random-cell), so the gains show query-guided retrieval beats
uniform temporal coverage at matched budget but do not fully isolate spatial
indexing from embedding similarity within cells.

\paragraph{HDD is a systems, not retrieval-accuracy, result.}
HDD ships no look-back QA labels, so its results (\S\ref{sec:results-hdd}) are
\emph{systems} and \emph{self-supervised}, not Hit@5/mIoU---a deliberate division
where the wearable corpora carry accuracy and HDD carries persistence. Two
caveats: the cross-session split (same $r{=}10$ cell vs.\ all others) blends
geographic proximity with visual identity, so a $k$-nearest-cell control is the
next step; and the features carry a ${\sim}3$\,s GPS-warmup skew (${\sim}17$\,m
median) corrected at analysis time (AUC $0.848{\to}0.853$), with the source-side
fix verified in simulation but not yet re-extracted.
